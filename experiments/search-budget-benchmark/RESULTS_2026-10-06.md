# Search Budget Falsification — 2026-10-06

## Evidence status

**Reproducible directional benchmark.** The committed runner and the figures below use the same deterministic seed formula and five landscapes per surface. The sample is intentionally small enough to replay cheaply; treat it as scheduler evidence, not a universal theorem about idea search.

## Question

The first cross-domain observation suggested replacing one 1,000-generation lineage with many shorter bloodlines. This follow-up tries to falsify that conclusion under a fixed evaluation budget.

## Comparison

Population is fixed at 64 with 12 survivors. Each strategy spends the same 1,000 generation-equivalents:

- 1000 × 1
- 500 × 2
- 200 × 5
- 100 × 10
- 50 × 20
- hybrid: 500 × 1 plus 50 × 10

Two deliberately different deterministic search surfaces are used.

### Rugged / deceptive surface

Five landscapes with local optima and pairwise interactions.

Observed mean best score:

- 1000 × 1: 48.732
- 500 × 2: 55.949
- 200 × 5: 56.685
- 100 × 10: 57.002
- 50 × 20: 59.394
- hybrid: 57.957

The short-restart strategy has the best mean on this small rugged sample. One long lineage is the weakest.

### Smooth / cumulative surface

Five deterministic 128-bit landscapes with a smooth gradient and a prefix reward that benefits sequential accumulation.

Observed mean best score:

- 1000 × 1: 192.000
- 500 × 2: 192.000
- 200 × 5: 192.000
- 100 × 10: 190.500
- 50 × 20: 152.100
- hybrid: 192.000

The 50-generation-only strategy fails badly. The hybrid retains the same mean as the long-lineage strategies.

## Revision

The earlier recommendation "100–200 generations per bloodline" was too broad, and the later idea that hybrid scheduling was simply best was also too strong.

The safer interpretation is:

1. rugged landscapes benefit strongly from independent restarts,
2. smooth cumulative landscapes need sufficiently long lineages,
3. when the landscape type is unknown, a **barbell allocation** is a robustness hedge rather than a proven optimum,
4. scheduler choice should itself be tested across multiple surfaces and seeds,
5. real idea-search policy must still be validated with real evaluators and evidence.

## Next falsification target

Run the same policies on reproducible real KODOKU idea genomes with multiple independent evaluator groups and measure:

- winner stability across evaluator variants,
- winner stability across seeds,
- lineage diversity,
- late-generation improvement rate,
- real-world evidence agreement.

If the same candidate wins only under one evaluator or one scheduler, it should not be promoted downstream.
